About the App

This study task management application helps students organise their academic responsibilities in one central place. You can create, update, delete, and view study tasks with ease. The goal is to reduce stress, improve organisation, and help you stay on top of your workload.

  • Create and manage study tasks
  • Track due dates and course information
  • Simple and user-friendly interface
  • Secure access with a basic login system
